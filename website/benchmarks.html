Evaluations

Spectrum GSE v1.0 is validated through deterministic test plans that emphasize reproducibility. No adaptive learning or control paths are used; all behaviors are fixed and documented.

Validation objectives

The evaluation program measures determinism, coverage of checks, and clarity of artifacts produced by Spectrum GSE.

Deterministic repeatability

Verify that identical inputs yield identical reports and artifacts across runs and environments.

Evidence fidelity

Confirm that generated manifests, hashes, and diffs are sufficient for academic or enterprise review without relying on heuristics.

Non-adaptive assurance

Demonstrate that no learning, tuning, or control loops are invoked during validation.

Planned scenarios

Deterministic evaluation scenarios spanning structured and randomized circuits, reported with reproducible manifests.

Structured circuits

GHZ-style chains and other structured topologies used to verify invariants, report stability of outputs, and validate instrumentation notes.

Randomized topologies

Random entangling ladders and parameterized networks used to confirm that documentation and manifests remain deterministic under varied layouts.

As evaluation artifacts are finalized, this page will link to reports and manifests in the /benchmarks/ directory. All outputs will retain deterministic, non-learning behavior.
